AI scams and deepfakes

Cloned voices and fake video calls.

Why it matters

Voices and faces can be faked. Cheap tools can clone a voice from a short clip.

Messages are more polished. AI removes the typos that used to give scams away.

Urgency still gives it away. Money, secrecy and pressure are the signs to watch.

A familiar voice isn’t proof.

The habits

  • Agree a code word. With family and finance teams.
  • Verify on a second channel. Call back on a known number.
  • Don’t trust spelling alone. Judge the request, not the polish.
  • Limit public voice and video. It can be used to copy you.

Test yourself

Two options each time. Pick the better one.
