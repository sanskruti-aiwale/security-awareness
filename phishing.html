Phishing and email

Spot fake messages before they work.

Why it matters

Email is the front door. It reaches everyone, costs attackers almost nothing, and needs only one click.

A click can hand over more than a password. Fake pages steal logins, and attachments can install malware.

Good ones look ordinary. Real logos and plausible stories make scams hard to spot by sight alone.

Pause. Hover. Verify.

Sort your inbox

    The habits

    • Check the sender. Read the full address, not the display name.
    • Hover before you click. The real destination shows on screen.
    • Distrust urgency. Deadlines are built to stop you thinking.
    • Verify another way. Call a number you already have.
