Data privacy

What you share and how you handle it.

Why it matters

Oversharing fuels scams. Job titles, birthdays and travel plans help attackers write believable messages.

Sensitive data needs care. Customer and company files belong only in approved places.

Small leaks add up. A wrong recipient or an open link can expose data in one click.

Share the minimum.

The habits

  • Share the minimum. Only what the task needs.
  • Check recipients. Read the To line before you send.
  • Use approved storage. Not personal email or drives.
  • Review privacy settings. On social media and your apps.

Test yourself

Two options each time. Pick the better one.
