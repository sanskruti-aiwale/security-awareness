When something goes wrong

Stop, report, recover.

Why it matters

Speed limits the damage. Reporting in minutes can stop a stolen password becoming a breach.

Hiding it makes it worse. Attackers keep access while a mistake stays quiet.

No blame. Teams that reward reporting hear about problems early.

Stop, report, recover.

The habits

  • Stop and don’t reply. Leave links and files alone.
  • Report straight away. Use the report button or tell IT.
  • Change passwords safely. From a clean device, then enable MFA.
  • Keep the evidence. Don’t delete it until IT says.

Test yourself

Two options each time. Pick the better one.
