Safe browsing and Wi-Fi

Fake sites, risky downloads and public networks.

Why it matters

Fake sites look real. Copied pages steal logins, and a padlock only means the connection is encrypted.

Public Wi-Fi is shared. On an open network, others nearby may snoop on or tamper with traffic.

Downloads can carry malware. Cracked software and free tools are common delivery routes.

Type it, don’t click it.

The habits

  • Type the address. Use bookmarks for banking and work sites.
  • Padlock isn’t trust. It shows encryption, not honesty.
  • Protect public Wi-Fi use. Use a VPN or your phone’s hotspot.
  • Download from official sources only. Skip cracked software.
